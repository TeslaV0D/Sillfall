Sillfall – privacy in short

Sillfall draws animated weather on top of your open windows.

What stays on this PC
- The app reads where windows are and how big they are. It never reads window titles or window contents, and it does not record the screen.
- Settings, the visitor collection and error reports are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sillfall. Error reports are not uploaded.

What is sent
- Nothing, unless you switch weather sync on in the settings.
- With weather sync on, a location rounded to about 11 km is sent to MET Norway (api.met.no) every 15 to 60 minutes. You can switch it off at any time.
- No telemetry, no advertising, no account.

Changes to your system
- Setup adds a Start menu entry and registers sillfall:// links. Autostart is off until you switch it on in the settings.
- Uninstall from Settings > Apps removes the app, the link registration and the autostart entry. Your settings stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sillfall until you delete that folder.

Full privacy policy: https://github.com/TeslaV0D/Sillfall/blob/main/docs/privacy.md
