# ADR-0006: Rain

- Status: accepted (visuals subject to maintainer sign-off)
- Date: 2026-11-11
- Milestone: M4

## Context

PLAN.md, section 4.1 describes rain: drops with depth, splashes, puddles, gutters, and waterfalls, plus procedural sound (section 8). Some points were left open in the plan; others emerged when reviewing rendered previews.

## Decisions

### 1. Depth and occlusion

Each drop has depth `z` from 0 to 1. Size, speed, length, and opacity grow with `z`. The “Depth” setting defines which fraction is close enough to interact with windows (`z ≥ 1 − Depth`). **Distant drops fall behind windows** and are not drawn where a window covers their tip. The first preview showed them over window content, which broke depth and hurt readability.

### 2. Puddles as a water field per window top edge

`WaterField`: a row of bins (4 px × DPI) across the full window width, stored window-local. Hits add water, diffusion evens out, slow creep drives water toward both corners, and evaporation drains it. Overflow and water reaching a corner become gutters. On resize the field is resampled with total volume preserved (tested).

After rain ends, water evaporates faster (~20 s). Once no bin holds visible water, the edge counts as dry. Without that rule, leftover runoff kept the effect and render loop alive indefinitely. A test caught that.

### 3. Gutters, splashes, waterfalls

- Gutters run just outside the frame downward and meander only outward, never over the window. Their path is a ring buffer of points drawn as stripe sprites. At the window bottom they fall freely.
- Splash droplets that would fall back to impact height disappear. Otherwise they fell into the window.
- From intensity 0.8, wet corners pour continuously (waterfall) as a stream of small near drops.
- Shake or close sheds the edge’s water as drops. Move carries gutters along.

### 4. DPI

`SimulationContext.PixelScale` is the primary monitor’s scale factor. Rain sizes, spacing, speeds, and bin width are multiplied by it. With mixed DPI across monitors the primary factor applies. Per-monitor scaling would be much heavier. Whether mixed DPI feels wrong is for sign-off.

### 5. Sound

`RainSynth` (Core) generates pink noise through a bandpass. Light rain sounds bright (up to 3.2 kHz), heavy fuller (up to 1.3 kHz) and broader. Drop ticks follow the simulation’s real impact rate. `RainSoundPlayer` (Audio) plays via XAudio2 with three 40 ms buffers. Sound is off by default. No audio device is reported, not a crash. Per-category volume settings arrive with the settings UI (M9).

### 6. Budget

12,000 drops, 2,000 splash droplets, 600 gutters with 12 path points each, and 600 ripples. `RenderFrame` reserves space for 32,768 sprites from the start and does not grow during play. 0 allocations per frame is tested.

## Consequences

- Visuals are a first pass after WARP preview review. Sign-off on real hardware remains per plan (`docs/manual-tests/M4.md`). Feedback updates parameters and goldens.
- New rain goldens are adopted only after visual review.
